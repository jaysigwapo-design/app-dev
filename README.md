# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame 🦸‍♂️

![Avengers Endgame](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0d/Avengers_Endgame_poster.jpg)

## 🎬 Overview
*Avengers: Endgame* is a 2019 superhero film produced by *Marvel Studios* and directed by *Anthony and Joe Russo*.  
It is the epic conclusion to the Infinity Saga, where the Avengers assemble once more to undo the catastrophic events caused by *Thanos*.

## 💥 Why I Like It
- It brings together all the heroes from the Marvel Cinematic Universe.  
- The movie perfectly balances action, emotion, and storytelling.  
- The teamwork and sacrifice theme make it unforgettable.  

## 🌟 Main Characters
1. Iron Man (Tony Stark)  
2. Captain America (Steve Rogers)  
3. Thor  
4. Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)  
5. Hulk (Bruce Banner)  
6. Hawkeye (Clint Barton)
